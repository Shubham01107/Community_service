LEARN DIGITAL SAFETY

Know the Threats
Before They Reach You.

Simple knowledge can prevent serious cyber fraud.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication wherever possible.

🔢

OTP & PIN Safety

Never share OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or CVV with anyone over calls or messages.

🎣

Phishing

Learn to identify fake emails, messages and websites designed to steal information.

💳

Safe Payments

Verify payment requests and never enter your UPI PIN just to receive money.

👁️

Online Privacy

Think carefully before sharing personal details, photos or documents online.

📱

Social Media Safety

Keep accounts private and avoid accepting unknown requests or sharing sensitive information.

COMMON THREATS

Scams You Should
Recognize Instantly.

01

Fake KYC

"Your account will be blocked. Update KYC now."

02

OTP Fraud

Someone pretends to be a bank employee and asks for your OTP.

03

UPI Fraud

Fake payment requests or QR codes are used to steal money.

04

Fake Jobs

"Pay registration fee and get a guaranteed job."

05

Prize Scam

Fake lottery or prize messages asking for processing fees.

06

Privacy Threat

Unknown people asking for private photos, documents or personal information.
